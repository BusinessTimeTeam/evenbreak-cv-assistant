### Video transcript: leading with feedback

Jane:

As a leader, it's really important to listen to feedback from your team. Even a leader with lived experience like myself, I only have lived experience of my own condition. I don't know what the barriers are that other people face, and so as a leader, I need to be open to hearing what barriers other people within my team and my business are facing, and what maybe I need to do to remove those barriers.

So listening to feedback is really important. One of the things I'm very, very grateful for at Evenbreak is that we only employ disabled people. So I get lots of feedback and it means that we can ensure that everything we do at Evenbreak is accessible and inclusive for all, because we cover pretty much every barrier that people can face.

And that means that I get feedback about things that I'm doing or feedback about things that Evenbreak is doing, which may prevent other people from really thriving and being as productive as they could be.

So I receive feedback regularly from my team on things like the language I might use, on things like the processes we have in place, on things like how do we communicate with each other? How do we make sure that we listen to every voice within Evenbreak, not just the loud ones.

And there are a few of those!

So I think it's really important as a leader to create a climate where it's fine to give feedback. And actually it's not just around disability, it's also feedback around decisions that we might make.

One of the real, um, benefits of Evenbreak is that we have a lot of neurodivergent people working within Evenbreak who will come up with ideas that I would never think of, in a million lifetimes, and I have to be open to thinking about suggestions about doing things in a way that perhaps I wouldn't think of. But actually when you think through them are really, really good. And so for me, a good leader is really open to learning from their team, whether that's about barriers and adjustments, whether it's about decisions, whether it's about how you produce your products or services in a better way.

Feedback is really their secret source to success, I feel.
