Website

Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA)The ADA is a federal civil rights law that prohibits disability discrimination in employment and other areas of public life. Employers are required to provide reasonable accommodations unless doing so would cause undue hardship.&nbsp;Follow the link to learn more.
