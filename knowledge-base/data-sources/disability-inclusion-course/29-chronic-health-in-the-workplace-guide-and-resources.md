### Promoting healthy work for workers with chronic illness: A guide to good practice

This guide from the&nbsp;European Network for Workplace Health Promotion (ENWHP)&nbsp;is for employers and managers who want to better support employees with chronic illness. It offers guidance on helping people stay in work or return after time off, and highlights the important contribution employees with chronic illness make.
