# Performance management stage 3: growth and development

### Stage 3: supporting growth and development equitably

Performance management should not only measure present performance; it should also create equitable opportunities for future growth.

Ensure employees can access training, mentoring and leadership programmes in a variety of formats—for example, videos, resources, peer upskilling sessions and coaching sessions.

**Create personal development plans.** Each person should have dedicated time away from their usual role to work on new skills and interests that contribute to career progression.

**Embed adjustments into development plans.** Growth opportunities should include practical strategies that account for fluctuating conditions, sensory needs or energy levels.

**Important:** ensure there are multiple types of progression and salary-enhanced pathways. Not everyone is suited to or interested in leadership, so progression should also be available through specialisation in skills or assets that are valuable to the company.
