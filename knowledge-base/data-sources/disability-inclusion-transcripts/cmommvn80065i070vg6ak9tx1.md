# Five everyday actions for disability inclusion

### Five everyday actions for disability inclusion

1. **Ask and listen.** Check in regularly and create safe spaces for colleagues to share their needs and experiences.
2. **Observe your surroundings.** Notice barriers such as noise, clutter or unclear documents, and suggest small improvements such as quiet spaces, bullet-point notes and agendas in advance.
3. **Create flexibility.** Adapt collaboration and communication to suit colleagues' diverse working styles.
4. **Challenge assumptions.** Speak up about biases, inaccessible practices or cultural norms that create barriers.
5. **Keep learning.** Engage in training, use available resources and learn from disabled voices and lived experience.
