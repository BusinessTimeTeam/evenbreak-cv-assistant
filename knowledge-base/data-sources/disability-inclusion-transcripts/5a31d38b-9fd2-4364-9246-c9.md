# Performance management stage 1: inclusive goals

### Stage 1: reviewing and setting goals inclusively

Performance management starts with clarity. For disabled employees, inclusive goal-setting means ensuring that expectations are realistic, flexible and tailored to access needs.

At the start of the review, ask how they have found the previous year, whether they achieved last year's goals, and about any challenges they feel prevented them from thriving. During the review, invite them to contribute proactively by suggesting goals, ideas and realistic timeframes. Goals are more achievable when the individual has co-created them.

By the end of the review, you should both be clear about the targets for the coming year. These should be clear, measurable, realistic and written down for both people to refer back to.

**Important:** measure whether the goal has been achieved, not how it was achieved. Instead of “must attend all in-person meetings”, for example, a goal could be “actively contribute to team discussions”. This permits remote or asynchronous participation and accounts for different needs and ways of working.
